WoundKit.fbx is generated by tools/wounds/build_wound_kit.py (made for this game).

The gore pieces (Gore_*) are built from anatomy taken from Z-Anatomy
(https://www.z-anatomy.com, https://github.com/LluisV/z-anatomy), licensed
CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/): the head, the
liver, the ribs, the shoulder and arm, the hips, the pelvis and the
intestines (see tools/wounds/anatomy/README.md). These derived pieces are
shared alike under the same licence, with this credit. The wound inserts, the
head shells and the frames are procedural.
