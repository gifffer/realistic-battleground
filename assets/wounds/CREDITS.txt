WoundKit.fbx is generated by tools/wounds/build_wound_kit.py (procedural,
made for this game), with real anatomy from Z-Anatomy:

  Z-Anatomy (https://www.z-anatomy.com, https://github.com/LluisV/z-anatomy)
  licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

The pieces made from it are shared alike under the same licence, with this
credit: the burst head's bones, brain, eyes, teeth, jaw and neck vertebrae,
the liver (Gore_*); the torn arm and shoulder, the humerus (Gore_ArmEnd_*,
Gore_Shoulder_*, Gore_Humerus*); the broken ribs in the Tear insert; the
chest the Sentinel's bore cuts through - heart, sternum, ribs and their
cartilage, pectoral and intercostal muscles, lung (Bore* inserts); the body
torn in two at the hips - lumbar vertebrae and discs, small intestine,
colon, abdominal and back muscles, hip bones, sacrum (Gore_Waist_*,
Gore_Hips_*, Gore_GutPile_*); the heavy hits - the thigh's muscles, femur,
hip bone and the muscles round the hip joint (Gore_LegEnd*_*, Gore_Hip*_*),
the radius and ulna, tibia and fibula of the open fractures
(Gore_ForearmBreak_*, Gore_ShinBreak_*), the teeth of the broken jaw
(Gore_JawBroken_*). What was taken and how: tools/wounds/anatomy/README.md.
