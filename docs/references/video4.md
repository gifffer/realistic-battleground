1. ATTACKS / ANIMATIONS
- There are no strikes on a target. Animation is pose-based: crouch anticipation (1.47 s), hand charge (1.7 s), held power pose, a slow turn to face the camera.
- Timing pattern: 1-frame white flash → 3–4 frames of anticipation → about 1.5 s of sustained effect. A long "hold" is used for drama.
- Camera: discrete cuts and jumps rather than smooth tracking (1.40 s, 1.47 s, 3.47 s zoom-punch, 16.6 s arena cut, 24.4 s cut). In the arena the camera is low, centred and symmetrical, framing the character between the giant bones and hands.

2. VFX
- Global colour grading is the main tool. Grey/desaturated world plus a saturated character in normal state. White-out transitions. Then a fully warm orange/amber grade with strong bloom and DOF in the "awakened domain".
- 1-frame overexposed flash with a purple chromatic fringe as the trigger.
- Neon glow lines: pink-red, thin, long, fading over about 1.5 s.
- Ground eruptions: an object bursts from the floor, leaves a black hole decal plus a radial spray of chunky orange/black shards and a dust puff. Staggered left to right, about 2–3 frames apart.
- Black ink smoke: large dark soft particles, rising slowly, lifetime of several seconds, wrapping the body.
- Glitch UI: white rectangles and jittery text, red shaky text for the punchline.
- Billboard UI elements in the world (orange glowing buttons) that get physically destroyed. This is diegetic UI used as a prop.
- There is no visible screen shake in the low-res frames (unsure; maybe small at 7.3–7.6 s during the eruptions).
- No damage numbers or hit markers (no combat).

3. GORE
- None. No blood, wounds, dismemberment or bodies. The red specks around 3.0–3.4 s are most likely glow particles, not blood (unsure). The black holes and scorch marks are the only "damage" decals, and they persist about 2+ s (holes) or until the end (curved scorch marks at 25–28 s).

5. KEY TAKEAWAYS (actionable, for awakening/ultimate cinematics, not for melee or gore)
- Start the awakening with a 1-frame (~67 ms) full-screen white overexposure flash with a purple/magenta edge fringe (ColorCorrection Brightness spike plus Bloom), then immediately cut the camera to a new angle.
- Desaturate the world (ColorCorrection Saturation about -0.8) while keeping the awakening character saturated, so he reads instantly. Remove it at the end.
- Anticipation: crouch pose for 3–4 frames, then a hand glow (bloom about 2–3 body-widths) for about 0.2 s before the main effect fires.
- Use an FOV punch / zoom-in of 1–2 frames (3.47 s) synced with a glitch overlay (white rectangles) to mark beats, then ease back out over about 0.3 s.
- Stage reveals one element at a time, about 0.2 s apart (buttons, words), each popping in over 2–3 frames with a slight overshoot.
- Destruction beats: objects burst from the ground in a left-to-right stagger, 2–3 frames apart. Each one spawns a black hole decal (kept for at least 2 s), a radial burst of 10–20 chunky orange/black shards with gravity, and a short grey dust puff.
- Black ink smoke for transformations: large dark soft particles, slow rise, 3–5 s lifetime, density high enough to hide the upper body, fading as the screen goes white.
- Domain change: fade exposure to white over about 3 s → warm flash → cut to a new environment with a strong orange/amber grade, dark top vignette, heavy bloom and DepthOfField (far blur). Frame it symmetrically with large glowing set pieces in the foreground.
- World-space kinetic text: tilted diagonal lines that fade in over 3–4 frames and hold 1.5–2 s. Use red, jittery glitch text for the final punchline.
- End with a 1-frame hard cut to white, then a 0.5–0.8 s fade back to the normal world. Leave persistent ground decals (scorch or claw marks) and floating props (hands) that bob, then fade out over about 0.6 s.
- For a realistic gritty game, borrow the techniques (flash frame, desaturate + selective colour, staggered ground eruptions with hole decals, smoke, exposure fades, FOV punch) but not the cartoon/Undertale theme. Keep the total cinematic much shorter (3–5 s) so it suits server-authoritative PvP.
