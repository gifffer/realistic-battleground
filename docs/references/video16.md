## 1. What it is
"A generic Roblox fight animation (Moon Animator)": a 17 s **animated cutscene**, not gameplay. Two R6 rigs fight on a dark tiled stage under a soft, cold top light, with a pale halo on the floor under them:
- **White**: white skin, orange shirt, angry face;
- **Blue**: classic yellow noob, blue shirt.

The camera is a free animated camera: a mostly side-on 3/4 view at chest height that follows the pair, with reframes and shakes.

The structure:
- **0–10 s**: a non-stop exchange of punches, a clinch, kicks and a heavy rally that ends in a clash that throws both fighters apart;
- **10–12.2 s**: a lull, wide, both in guard, breathing;
- **12.2–12.55 s**: both dash in;
- **12.60–13.63 s**: a hard cut to a slow-motion low close-up with rim lights (cyan on Blue, warm on White). White's fist comes into the lens;
- **13.67 s**: a full white frame, then a linear fade to black (about 0.85 s), then black until 17 s.

There is no SFX analysis here (we don't touch sound now). There is a "Nullscript" watermark.

The style is anime/cartoon: white starburst sprites, a "clover" smoke core, lightning crackles and white crescents. **Take the timing, the layering, the size and how short it all is. Not the cartoon shapes.**

| Blood after | **stains on the struck part and on the attacker's fist** | – | dark red | stay for seconds |

**Screen-level effects:**
- **There is no full-screen flash on normal hits.** The flash is local; whole-frame brightness rises only about 1 point.
- **Heavy hits: the screen DIMS before the contact.**
  - On the strike frame (1–2 frames before contact) the whole frame goes about **-27 % darker on average and -45 % at the edges** (a vignette that clamps down).
  - It usually comes with a camera reframe or push-in.
  - The burst then lands in a dark frame, and the brightness **recovers linearly over 8–10 frames (≈0.3 s)**.
  - This happened on all 5 hits of the final rally. The climax held it longest (10 frames).
- **Camera shake** on heavy contacts: 2–4 frames of jitter.
- **Blocked hits:**
  - Hits on the guard give at most a small warm flash with no smoke core and no blood (f5, f50, f217, f228).
  - A combo that is fully blocked or missed (6.07–6.40 s) shows **nothing**.

**Victim reactions:**
- **light**: the head or torso snaps back 15–25° in 1 frame, plus a one-step stagger;
- **heavy**: thrown 1–2 studs back at once, the torso folded 30–40°, the arms flung out, the feet sliding;
- **climax**: both bodies are blown about 6+ studs apart in under 0.2 s.

There is never any ease-in into a hit.

## 4. The camera
- An orbiting side-on 3/4 view at chest height that follows the pair, with gentle drift.
- **Hard reframes or push-ins on the frames of heavy strikes** (they come together with the dimming).
- Shake on heavy contact.
- After the climax, a pull-out to a wide shot held 2 s.
- The finisher: a hard cut to a **low close-up in slow motion** (about 1 s), the vignette closing in, the fist going into the lens, then a **white frame (2 fr at full white) fading linearly to black in about 0.85 s**.

## 5. The punch animation: key poses for rebuilding R6 clips
Measured on White's heavy straight (f243–f258), the one-two (f40–f51), the jabs (f3–f5, f13–f15), the overhand (f199–f208), the uppercut (f55–f59) and the spinning back-fist (f62–f70).

Frames are given at **60 fps** (source frames × 2). Angles are estimated from the silhouettes.
- **Torso yaw** is about the vertical axis, + toward the punching side's target (the punching shoulder goes forward).
- **Lean** + = forward.
- **Shoulder flex**: 0° = arm hanging, 90° = arm horizontal forward, 180° = straight up.
- **Adduction** + = toward and across the centreline.
- **Hip flex** + = thigh forward.
- **Root** offsets are in studs (forward, up).

**Style notes** (what makes these punches feel strong):
- **Very little anticipation, then a near-instant strike.** 1–2 source frames from the load to full extension. The load sits on a held pose, not an ease.
- **The whole body goes in:** a big torso yaw (about 60° in all), a lunge of the root, the rear leg straight behind, the head counter-rotating to keep the eyes on the target.
- **The non-punching arm always does something:** it goes back to the chin on straights and is flung back on haymakers. That counter-motion sells the power.
- **The guard is high and hunched** with a constant small bob; the stance is very wide (R6 legs about ±20–30°).
- **Combos alternate the hands** and shift the weight forward step by step; each blow starts from where the last one ended, without going back to neutral.
- **The victim reacts on the contact frame itself** (a snap of 15–25°), and the attacker holds the contact pose for the hitstop.

## 6. Key takeaways for our realistic style
1. **Keep the 4-beat grammar, rendered realistically:**
   - F-1: a tiny warm-white glow and point light at the contact point;
   - F0: a pop of a hot flash (realistic: sweat and skin moisture spray, dust shaken off the clothes) plus 1–3 thin motion streaks along the strike line;
   - F+1: small shards (droplets, specks) breaking outward;
   - F+2…: a grey dust or breath puff that drifts and fades in 0.3–0.5 s.
   - **No** white starburst sprite, clover smoke or lightning crackle.
2. **The effects are small and short.** The burst is 1.5–2.5 studs and lives 1 frame; the whole hit is under 0.5 s. That is why it reads as "snappy" and not as particles everywhere.
3. **Dim, don't flash, for heavy hits.**
   - A vignette or exposure dip (about -25 %, edges -45 %) 1–2 frames before contact, recovering over 0.3 s.
   - It suits our dark, gritty grading much better than white flashes.
   - Keep the full white frame for the KO or finisher only.
4. **Air-displacement arcs on medium and heavy hits** become, for us, a faint see-through pressure ring or wind-dust wisps round the victim (0.2–0.3 s), not white anime crescents.
5. **Three intensities, coherent with the hit:**
   - **blocked**: a small warm spark or none, no blood, no shake;
   - **clean**: flash + spray + streaks + dust;
   - **heavy / last hit**: dim + push-in + shake + blood, then stains that stay.
   - Fully blocked or missed swings: **nothing**.
6. **Blood timing and colour already match our rules:**
   - a flat dark-maroon splash (104, 15, 11) that breaks into lace blobs, then flecks within 0.2 s;
   - stains stay on both the victim and the **attacker's fist**. Add knuckle blood to the attacker after blood hits.
7. **Rhythm:** clusters of 2–3 hits 0.13–0.2 s apart, then resets; an accelerating heavy rally; a calm lull after the climax.
8. **Punch clips:** rebuild M1 from section 5:
   - jab → cross → hook or uppercut → overhand ender;
   - 2–4 frame (60 fps) loads, 2–4 frame strikes, a heavy hitstop of about 4 frames;
   - a 60° torso swing and a root lunge of about 0.5 stud;
   - the off arm countering.
   - For the realistic look, tone the cartoon down: lunge 0.4 instead of 0.6, stance ±20° instead of ±30°.
